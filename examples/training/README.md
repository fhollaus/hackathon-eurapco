# Public training tickets

15 independent initial requests, progressing from easy to more complex messages.
They are examples to explore, not a final scoring set. Each new execution starts
from the same fixture state. Replies belong to the original ticket/run.

During development you decide what to answer, including invalid or insufficient
values, without rewriting supplied facts. The baseline does not yet resume these
replies; implementing that is part of your challenge. Templates are in ../events/.
